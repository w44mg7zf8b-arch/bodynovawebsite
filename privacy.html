Privacy Policy

How BodyNova Beauty Center collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or our clinic.

Last updated: 13 May 2026

1. Introduction

BodyNova Beauty Center ("BodyNova", "we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you use our website or book a treatment with our clinic.

By using this website or submitting any form on it, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

When you use our website we may collect the following information, only when you choose to provide it:

  • Contact details — your full name, phone number, and email address, submitted through our booking and contact forms.
  • Appointment details — the treatment you are interested in, your preferred date and time, and any notes you choose to share.
  • Technical information — basic browser and device information that is automatically sent when you visit any website (for example IP address, browser type, and the pages you view), used only to keep the site secure and performant.

We do not collect payment-card information through this website. All payment for treatments is taken in clinic, in person.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and confirm your appointment with our clinic team.
  • Send you booking confirmations and important treatment-related messages.
  • Personalise the consultation and treatment plan we recommend for you.
  • Improve our website, security, and overall service.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is only accessible to authorised BodyNova staff who need it to handle your booking or enquiry. We may share information with trusted service providers (for example our website host or email service) only to the extent necessary to operate the site, and only under appropriate confidentiality protections.

We may disclose information where legally required to do so under applicable laws of the United Arab Emirates.

5. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies and storage required for the site to function correctly (for example, to remember your selections on a single visit). We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features of the site may not work as expected if you do.

6. Data retention

We keep your enquiry and appointment details for as long as needed to provide our services to you and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct any information that is inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information from our records.
  • Withdraw your consent to future communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorised access, and misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our services and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, with the "Last updated" date at the top revised accordingly.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your information? Email our team or call our clinic.
