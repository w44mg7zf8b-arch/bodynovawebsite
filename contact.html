Before you visit

Common questions, quickly answered

How do I book my first appointment?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or fill in the form above. Our team will recommend the best treatment for your goals and confirm an available slot — usually within an hour during clinic hours.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Every first visit starts with a short consultation so we can understand your skin, body goals, and comfort. There's no pressure — we'll only suggest treatments that suit you.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), cash, Tabby installments, and Apple Pay at the clinic.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Of course. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice so we can offer your slot to another client. Reschedule via WhatsApp or by calling the clinic.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Yes — free guest parking is available at the building. The clinic entrance is step-free and easy to access.
