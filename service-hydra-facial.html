Treatment Overview

What is Hydra Facial?

Hydra Facial at Body Nova is a refreshing skin treatment that deeply cleanses, hydrates, and revitalizes the complexion. It is designed for clients who want smoother, fresher, and more radiant-looking skin in a comfortable clinic setting.

The treatment combines gentle exfoliation with targeted hydration so your skin feels clean without feeling stripped. Each session is tailored to your skin's current condition and goals.

It is suitable for most skin types and is a popular choice before events, after travel, or as a regular monthly skin reset.

  • Deep cleansing and exfoliation
  • Targeted hydration
  • Suitable for most skin types
  • Refreshed, glowing finish

Common questions

Things you might be wondering

Is this treatment surgical?

No. Hydra Facial is a non-surgical aesthetic clinic treatment, planned around your goals and delivered by our team with comfort-focused care.

How long does a session take?

Most sessions are scheduled for around 45 to 75 minutes. We confirm exact timing during your consultation, based on the treatment plan that suits you.

When can I return to daily activities?

Most clients return to their day right after the session. Your therapist will share simple aftercare guidance to support your results.

How do I know if this is right for me?

We start with a friendly consultation. Our team will review your goals and recommend whether Hydra Facial or a different treatment is the best fit.
